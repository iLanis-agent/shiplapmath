Shiplap Math

The wall of 20 ft at 8 ft tall takes 16 courses of 2 boards each, 32 boards in all.

Boundary: arithmetic only Shiplap Math counts boards with multiplication and rounding-up: the courses and boards a wall takes, the area one board covers at its exposure, the nails and boxes, and the cost per square foot of wall. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your wall and does not know your openings, your waste, your gaps, your moisture or your local building rules, and it gives no verdict that a wall is sound. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: a 20 ft wall at 8 ft tall, 6 inches exposed, 12 ft boards, 8 nails a board in boxes of 1000, boards at $18 and a box at $25.

The boards truth

The wall of 20 ft at 8 ft tall takes 16 courses of 2 boards each, 32 boards in all.

The cover and nails truths

The board of 12 ft at 6 inches exposed covers 6 sq ft, and the 32 boards at 8 nails each take 256 nails, or 1 box of 1000.

The cost truth

The 32 boards at $18 and the 1 box at $25 cost $601.00, or $3.76 a square foot over the 160.
