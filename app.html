Shiplap Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts boards with multiplication and rounding-up: the courses and boards a wall takes, the area one board covers at its exposure, the nails and boxes, and the cost per square foot of wall. The lengths, the exposure, the nail counts and the prices are yours to measure or read off your own bundle label. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your wall and does not know your openings, your waste, your gaps, your moisture or your local building rules, and it gives no verdict that a wall is sound. Every number here is your own entry.

The boards truth

Courses and boards: the wall divided by the exposure and the board length, each rounded up.

The cover truth

Cover: the exposure times the board length, in square feet.

The nails truth

Nails: the boards times the nails each, and the boxes rounded up.

The cost truth

Cost: the boards and boxes at their prices, and what that is per square foot of wall.
